Puppy PanicToilet Training Made Simple

When to take your puppy out

Toilet training is about timing. The more often your puppy goes in the right place and gets rewarded, the faster they learn.

After wakingFrom every nap, not just in the morning.
After eatingUsually within 15 to 30 minutes of a meal.
After playExcitement fills the bladder fast.
Every 1 to 2 hoursIn between, as a baseline.
Rule of thumb: a puppy can hold on for roughly their age in months plus one, in hours. A 2-month-old puppy needs a break about every 3 hours, even overnight.

Frequently asked questions

Is the guide really free?

Yes. It's a free resource for new puppy owners. There's nothing to pay and no card required.

Is it suitable for my breed?

Yes. The routines and training methods in the guide work for puppies of every breed and size, from Chihuahuas to Great Danes.

My puppy is already a few months old. Is it too late?

Not at all. The training steps and problem-solving pages work at any age. Start with the chapter that matches your biggest challenge.

Why does my puppy bite so much?

Biting is a normal part of puppy development and teething. The guide explains how to redirect it to toys and teach gentle mouths without punishment.

Do I need to buy anything?

No. Everything in the guide can be done with treats, a few toys and a little patience. Any other resources we mention are completely optional.
