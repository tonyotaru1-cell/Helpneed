Why I Document Instead Of Guess

I paid an agency three thousand dollars to "optimize" my first store. Six weeks later, traffic was flat and my inbox had a lot of screenshots of dashboards that meant nothing. I paid for two more tools after that, both cancelled within a month, both useless for what I actually needed.

What finally worked came from doing the boring parts myself. Setting up the Google Business Profile by hand. Reading the actual Shopify analytics instead of a summary someone else wrote. Testing one AI workflow at a time instead of buying a bundle of twelve I'd never open.

Once something worked, I wrote down exactly what I did and what it changed. That habit turned into this site. I am not selling a shortcut. I am showing you the notes so you don't have to pay the agency I paid, or waste the month I wasted, to learn the same lesson.

Test first, then teach it. That's the whole method.
, Femi
