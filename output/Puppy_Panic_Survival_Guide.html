Welcome To Puppy Parenthood 🐾

Congratulations on welcoming a new puppy into your life. The first weeks are important because your puppy is learning about your home, your routine and your expectations.

Many puppy behaviours that frustrate owners are normal parts of development. With patience, consistency and the right guidance, you can build a strong relationship with your dog.

The Puppy Panic Promise:
Help owners understand their dogs, create better habits and enjoy the journey.
PUPPY PANIC3

Preparing Your Home

  • Safe sleeping area
  • Food and water bowls
  • Chew toys
  • Training treats
  • Leash and collar
  • Remove dangerous objects (cables, shoes, cleaning products)

Remember

Your puppy learns from the environment you create.

PUPPY PANIC4

Understanding Puppy Behaviour

Why Puppies Bite

Puppies bite because they explore, play and experience teething. The goal is not punishment. The goal is teaching better choices.

Why Puppies Chew

Chewing is natural. Provide safe alternatives and reward good decisions.

Solving Common Problems

Puppy Crying At Night

  • Create a bedtime routine
  • Provide comfort
  • Allow toilet breaks

Puppy Jumping

Reward calm greetings instead of excited behaviour.

PUPPY PANIC9

Bonus: 7-Day Puppy Training Challenge

Five to ten minutes a day. Tick each day off as you go.

DayFocusDone
1Name game: say their name, reward eye contact
2Sit: lure with a treat, reward the moment they sit
3Handling: gently touch paws and ears, reward calm
4Come: short recalls indoors, big rewards
5Leave it: reward looking away from a treat in your hand
6Settle: reward lying calmly on their bed
7Review: practise every skill in a new room
